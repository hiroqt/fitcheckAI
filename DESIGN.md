# FitCheck and Floating Brand

## Composition

Use the supplied FitCheck shirt-scan logo as the source for the electric blue, white, and bold sans-serif identity. FitCheck identifies the product in navigation and calls to action. Floating Brand identifies the featured fashion label.

Frame the hero as a midnight-indigo editorial stage. Keep navigation at the top, set the label in oversized type behind the floating garment, and put the short message and primary action where the garment silhouette leaves room. Use the supplied layout reference for layering and scale. On mobile, reduce the overlap and keep the headline and action in the reading order. Decorative background type stays out of the accessibility tree when it repeats a visible label.

## Color and typography

- Keep the hero deep indigo with white foreground type. Use electric blue for key actions, focus, and garment accents. Place supporting sections on cool off-white with near-black headings and readable secondary text.
- Set headlines in a bold display sans-serif. Use a clean sans-serif for body text and controls. Keep line lengths short, and use tabular figures where a timer or price benefits from aligned digits.
- Use solid text colors. Check contrast where copy crosses the hero art. Keep small labels and form hints fully readable.

## Sections and controls

Give each section one job. Separate content with space and thin dividers. Use panels sparingly and reserve small corner radii for useful grouping. Keep key actions short and specific. Avoid eyebrows, background grids, drop shadows, decorative badges, and dense blocks of copy.

Use the viewfinder motif around the garment or preview where it communicates framing. Keep a visual-only preview disclosure near the preview controls and direct shoppers to the seller’s size chart. Use Philippine pesos for pilot prices.

## Motion and depth

Use garment lighting, pose, and overlap to establish depth. Keep incidental motion subtle and finite. Avoid pulsing, animated hover text, and scroll effects that obscure content. Use clear, stable hover and keyboard focus states. Honor reduced motion with a readable static composition and keep the hero usable if an interactive preview is unavailable.

The hero jacket is illustrative.
